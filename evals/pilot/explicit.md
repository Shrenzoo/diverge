# Next week’s shift-desk trial

For every last-minute vacancy next week, invite volunteers to offer a specific time window privately. You decide who covers each part and send each person their assignment. Volunteers can participate by phone or text using the tools the desk already has.

1. **Announce the opening.** Give the shift a short ID. Post its date and hours in the usual chat. Reach volunteers who use only the phone by private call or text. For example, “S4, Saturday, Nov 28, 9 am–noon. Call or text the desk with ‘S4’ and the hours you can cover. Please wait for my confirmation before coming in.”
