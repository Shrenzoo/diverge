# Next week’s shift-desk trial

For every last-minute vacancy next week, invite volunteers to offer a specific time window privately. You decide who covers each part and send each person their assignment. Volunteers can participate by phone or text using the tools the desk already has.

1. **Announce the opening.** Give the shift a short ID. Post its date and hours in the usual chat. Reach volunteers who use only the phone by private call or text. For example, “S4, Saturday, Nov 28, 9 am–noon. Call or text the desk with ‘S4’ and the hours you can cover. Please wait for my confirmation before coming in.”
2. **Record offers in one place.** In the shared sheet, record the shift ID, hours, time received and a temporary offer number. Keep names, numbers and the link between a person and their offer on the coordinator’s phone. Acknowledge each offer privately as pending.
3. **Resolve overlap and gaps.** If two offers together would cover the same hours twice, choose the combination that covers the whole shift with the fewest handoffs. Between equally suitable offers, use the first received. Flip a coin for an exact tie. If only 9–10 am is offered, ask that volunteer to stand by and request 10 am–noon. Keep the shift open while there’s a gap. If it reaches start time with a gap, tell the volunteer and flag the uncovered hours to the person running the shift.
