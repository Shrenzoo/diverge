# Next week’s trial for filling vacant shifts

Keep posting vacancies in group chat. For one week, have volunteers offer coverage privately by phone or text, and have one coordinator confirm each assignment.

1. **Open the shift.** Create a row in the shared spreadsheet with a shift ID, the date, the hours needed, and the status “Open.” Post those details in the group and ask volunteers to call or text the coordinator with the shift ID and their exact available hours. Keep names, numbers, and messages on the coordinator’s phone.
2. **Resolve competing offers.** Work through offers in the order they reach the phone. If two offers for the same hours arrive together and their order is unclear, flip a coin. Ask the first volunteer to hold the time while coverage is checked. Ask the other if they can take any remaining hours. Otherwise, offer standby and tell them to wait for private confirmation before coming in.
3. **Check the whole shift.** If someone offers only the first hour, record that block as pending and post the exact hours still needed. When offers cover the entire shift, check that each volunteer is still available and that the handoff leaves no gap. Call or text each person to confirm their exact block. Then mark the shift “Confirmed,” release standbys, and close the group post. If a gap remains at shift start, release pending holds, record the vacancy as unfilled, and update the group so everyone knows the shift is still open.
