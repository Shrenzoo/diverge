# Shift desk

A volunteer coordinator fills last-minute vacancies by posting in group chat. Volunteers sometimes claim the same shift. Some can cover only the first hour. The coordinator has a shared spreadsheet and a phone. A volunteer can call or text but cannot be required to create an account. There is no budget for another service.

A shift is confirmed only when the coordinator has checked coverage and sent the volunteer a confirmation. Contact details stay private. Propose one change the team can trial next week. Include what happens with two simultaneous offers and incomplete coverage.
